About Lëtter

Who we are Vienna & Luxembourg

A Digital Humanities master's student at the University of Vienna, building Lëtter to help Luxembourg residents understand their own official letters.

Together for the people who live here.

What this is

An independent research project that reads and explains official Luxembourg documents and paperwork.

Not an official service

Lëtter is not affiliated with the Luxembourg government and does not replace official correspondence.

Partnerships & pilots

How it works

SCAN

Point your camera at whatever you don't understand

A letter, a poster, the small print on a form — Lëtter reads it straight off the page, however it reached you.

UNDERSTAND

Every line explained, in your language

Deadlines, amounts, and what you actually need to do — laid out clearly, next to the original text so you can always check it.

ASK

Still unsure? Just ask

Chat with Lëtter about anything in the document, in plain language, until it actually makes sense — every answer can be read aloud too, for anyone who finds that easier than reading.
